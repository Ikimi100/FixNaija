About us

The story of a people's movement.

FixNaija is not a political party, a slogan, or a moment. It is a nationwide repair project — built by ordinary Nigerians, organized street by street, ward by ward, until no community is left behind.

Read our story → Meet the 2027 ticket
Our story

Built from the ground up

FixNaija was founded in 2025 on a simple conviction: Nigeria's problems are not a mystery — they are a maintenance failure. Roads, schools, clinics, power, security, trust between citizen and state — all of it can be repaired. But repair work does not begin in the corridors of power. It begins at the polling unit, on the street where you live.

The name is deliberate. Not "Save Naija." Not "Dream Naija." FixNaija — because fixing is practical work. It means showing up, counting what was promised, checking what was delivered, and refusing to look away when the two don't match.

We are not bound by party lines. Our loyalty is to the Nigerian people — every farmer in Kebbi, every trader in Onitsha, every student in Ibadan. Whatever your background, language, or political history, if you believe Nigeria can work, you already belong here.

Today, the movement spans all 36 states and the FCT, organized down to the individual polling unit — a structure built so that no Nigerian community, however remote, is ever invisible again.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where every citizen has a voice, leadership is accountable, and no LGA is left behind.

We envision a country where governance is transparent from the ward council to the federal cabinet, where public money is traceable by the public, and where development reaches every corner of the federation — not just the cities that make the news.

Our Mission

To mobilize, connect, and empower Nigerians through grassroots community networks across all 774 LGAs.

We build local structures that train, inform, and activate citizens to participate in governance, track public spending, monitor projects in their own communities, and advocate for their rights — with the tools and the network to be heard.

What we stand for

The six pillars of FixNaija

01

Community Organizing

Building ward-level structures that empower citizens to lead change from their own neighborhoods — because nobody knows a community's needs better than the people who live there.

02

Policy Accountability

Tracking government promises and public spending to ensure every kobo works for the people — from the federal budget down to the LGA allocation.

03

Youth Empowerment

Investing in the next generation of leaders through training, mentorship, and civic engagement — turning frustration into organized, constructive power.

04

Project Tracking

Monitoring public projects from budget line to commissioning, with community members reporting progress — or abandonment — from the project site itself.

05

Civic Education

Teaching citizens their rights, how governance works, and how to use their voice and their vote — because an informed citizen is impossible to ignore.

06

Unity Across Lines

Refusing the politics of ethnicity, religion, and division. A pothole does not check your tribe before it damages your car — and neither do we before we welcome you.

How we're organized

From the nation to your street

  1. 1National Network

    Sets the movement's direction, coordinates campaigns, and connects every state structure into one national voice.

  2. 36 + FCTState Chapters

    Every state of the federation and the Federal Capital Territory, each with its own coordinators adapting the mission to local realities.

  3. 774LGA Networks

    The heart of the movement. Every Local Government Area has its own community, where members organize, meet, and act together.

  4. 8,800+Ward Cells

    Close enough to know every street. Ward cells track local projects, welcome new members, and carry community concerns upward.

  5. 176,000+Polling Units

    The smallest unit of Nigerian democracy — and the foundation of FixNaija. When you register, you are counted right down to your own polling unit.

The Convener · For President

Prince Adewole Adebayo

Born on 8 January 1972 in Ondo City, Ondo State, Prince Adewole Adebayo is a prince of the Ondo Kingdom, a lawyer, and an entrepreneur whose career has been defined by building institutions rather than chasing office.

He studied law at Obafemi Awolowo University and was called to the Nigerian Bar in 2000, later earning additional qualifications abroad, including admission to the New York Bar. In 2002 he founded his own practice, Adewole Adebayo & Co. (House of Law), which grew into one of Nigeria's notable firms — and a training ground where he invested in young lawyers at the start of their careers.

In 2016, he returned his businesses home to Ondo City and founded KAFTAN TV, an independent 24-hour television channel now operating from Ondo with offices in Lagos and Abuja. Beyond business, he is known for sponsoring the education of young Nigerians in tertiary institutions across the country.

He was the Social Democratic Party's presidential candidate in 2023, and in May 2026 was again chosen as the SDP's candidate for the 2027 elections. FixNaija is the grassroots expression of his core message: Nigeria is not beyond repair — it is simply waiting for its people to organize.
