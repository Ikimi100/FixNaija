Meet the Candidate

Prince Adewole Adebayo

SDP Presidential Candidate · Prince of Ondo Kingdom · Lawyer

Born on 8 January 1972 in Ondo City, Prince Adewole Adebayo is a prince of the Ondo Kingdom, a lawyer, and an entrepreneur whose career has been defined by building institutions rather than chasing office. As the SDP Presidential Candidate, he now carries that record onto the national stage.

From founding his own legal practice to backing education, enterprise and social causes, his life's work has centred on lasting structures that outlive any one person. FixNaija channels those decades of institution-building into a citizen-owned movement to repair Nigeria across all 774 LGAs — grounded in accountability, competence and service to the ordinary Nigerian.
