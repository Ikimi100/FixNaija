Our Movement

A people-powered repair of Nigeria.

FixNaija is a citizen-owned grassroots movement dedicated to restoring trust in governance and ensuring that every Nigerian — in every ward, LGA and state — has access to quality services, infrastructure, opportunity and representation.

36 states + FCT. 774 communities.
One movement.Organised ward by ward — and growing every day.
What we stand for

Beyond politics.

  • AccountabilityLeaders and institutions answer for their promises and their stewardship of public resources.
  • ParticipationResidents take an active role in governance and decision-making — ward by ward.

    Why We Organise

    The challenges facing Nigerians.

    Across all 36 states and the FCT, families confront the same daily struggles — and FixNaija exists to repair them.

    ⚡

    Power & Energy

    A national grid of barely ~5,500MW for over 220 million people keeps homes dark and factories running on generators.

    🛡

    Insecurity

    Kidnapping, banditry and insurgency keep farmers off their fields and travellers afraid of their own highways.

    ⚖️

    Corruption

    Trillions of naira leak from public coffers every year — the hospital never built, the road never tarred, the teacher never paid.

    💼

    Unemployment

    Millions of capable young Nigerians are idle or underemployed, and the most talented are leaving in droves.

    💰

    Cost of Living

    Inflation has eaten salaries and savings, with roughly 63% of Nigerians living below the national poverty line.

    🎓

    Education

    Around 20 million Nigerian children are out of school — the highest number anywhere in the world.

    🏥

    Healthcare

    Hospitals without medicine, clinics without doctors, and a brain-drain shipping our best medics abroad.

    🛣

    Infrastructure

    Roughly 70% of the road network is unpaved or in disrepair — raising the price of everything that moves.

    Our commitment

    Every challenge is an invitation to repair. Through transparent governance, active citizen participation and the plans in the FixNaija Agenda, we are committed to a Nigeria that is safe, prosperous, inclusive and resilient — built ward by ward.
