Who We Are

A Movement Built Around the People

FixNaija is a citizen-owned grassroots movement dedicated to restoring trust in governance and ensuring that every Nigerian — in every ward, LGA and state — has access to quality services, infrastructure, opportunity and representation.

⚖️

Accountability to the People

Leaders and public institutions must answer for their actions, their promises and their stewardship of public resources.

🗳️

Citizen Participation

We organize residents to take an active role in governance, community development, policy discussion and public decision-making — ward by ward.

🔍

Transparency in Governance

We demand open decision-making, public access to information and leadership that earns the trust of citizens.

🌱

Sustainable Development

We champion repairs that last — institutions, infrastructure and opportunity built to outlive any one administration.

    Why We Organize

    The Challenges Facing Nigerians

    Building a better Nigeria begins with understanding the realities facing its people. Across all 36 states and the FCT, families confront the same daily struggles — and FixNaija exists to repair them.

    ⚡ Power & Energy

    A national grid of barely ~5,500MW for over 220 million people keeps homes dark and factories running on generators.

    🛡 Insecurity

    Kidnapping, banditry and insurgency keep farmers off their fields and travellers afraid of their own highways.

    ⚖️ Corruption

    Trillions of naira leak from public coffers every year — the hospital never built, the road never tarred, the teacher never paid.

    💼 Unemployment

    Millions of capable young Nigerians are idle or underemployed, and the most talented are leaving in droves.

    💰 Cost of Living

    Inflation has eaten salaries and savings, with roughly 63% of Nigerians living below the national poverty line.

    🎓 Education

    Around 20 million Nigerian children are out of school — the highest number anywhere in the world.

    🏥 Healthcare

    Hospitals without medicine, clinics without doctors, and a brain-drain shipping our best medics abroad.

    🛣 Infrastructure

    Roughly 70% of the road network is unpaved or in disrepair — raising the price of everything that moves.

    Our Commitment

    At FixNaija, every challenge is an invitation to repair. Through transparent governance, active citizen participation and the plans in the FixNaija Agenda, we are committed to a Nigeria that is safe, prosperous, inclusive and resilient — built ward by ward, hand in hand with residents, community leaders and partners.

    Meet the Candidate

    Prince Adewole Adebayo

    SDP Presidential Candidate · Prince of Ondo Kingdom · Lawyer

    Born on 8 January 1972 in Ondo City, Prince Adewole Adebayo is a prince of the Ondo Kingdom, a lawyer, and an entrepreneur whose career has been defined by building institutions rather than chasing office. As the SDP Presidential Candidate, he now carries that record onto the national stage.

    From founding his own legal practice to backing education, enterprise and social causes, his life's work has centred on lasting structures that outlive any one person. FixNaija channels those decades of institution-building into a citizen-owned movement to repair Nigeria across all 774 LGAs — grounded in accountability, competence and service to the ordinary Nigerian.

    The Running Mate

    Usman M. Bugaje

    SDP Vice-Presidential Candidate · Katsina State

    A former federal lawmaker who describes himself as an Almajiri, Usman M. Bugaje represented Jibia/Kaita Federal Constituency in the House of Representatives (2003–2007) and served as Special Adviser (Political) to the Vice President.

    In 2026 he chaired the planning committee of the SDP national convention in Bauchi, and was then nominated to stand beside Prince Adebayo — completing a South–North ticket committed to cutting the cost of governance.

    “We are not going to do the usual politics, which is the politics of big men.”— Usman M. Bugaje, SDP running mate
