About Us

A Movement Built From the Ground Up

FixNaija is a nationwide grassroots network built on the belief that real change starts in the community — not in the corridors of power. We organize at the ward and LGA level, training citizens to hold leaders accountable, monitor public projects, and demand transparency at every level of government.

We are not bound by party lines. Our loyalty is to the Nigerian people — every farmer in Kebbi, every trader in Onitsha, every student in Ibadan. FixNaija exists to give every citizen a seat at the table, regardless of background, language, or political affiliation.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where every citizen has a voice, leadership is accountable, and no LGA is left behind.

We envision a country where governance is transparent from the ward council to the federal cabinet, and where development reaches every corner of the federation.

Our Mission

To mobilize, connect, and empower Nigerians through grassroots community networks across all 774 LGAs.

We build local structures that train, inform, and activate citizens to participate in governance, track public spending, and advocate for their rights.

Our Driving Force

Guided by integrity, collective action, and the determination to reshape our nation.
