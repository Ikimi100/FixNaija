Privacy Policy

How the FixNaija Movement collects, uses and protects the information you share with us — written in plain language, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

The short version

  • We only collect what you choose to give us, to organise the movement and stay in touch with you.
  • We never sell your data, and we never share it with anyone for advertising.
  • Joining FixNaija shows that you support the movement. We treat that — and any religion or ethnic group you tell us — as sensitive information and use it only with your consent.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing fixnaijamovement@gmail.com.

01Who we are

The FixNaija Movement (“FixNaija”, “we”, “us”) is a citizen-owned grassroots movement organised across Nigeria's 36 states, the FCT and 774 Local Government Areas, rallying behind Prince Adewole Adebayo, Presidential Candidate of the Social Democratic Party (SDP), and his running mate Usman M. Bugaje.

We are responsible for the personal data collected through this website. You can reach us at fixnaijamovement@gmail.com (Abuja, FCT, Nigeria).

WhereWhat you give us
RegistrationFirst and last name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email, gender, marital status, age group, occupation, religion, ethnic group, membership status, digital and mobilisation skills, state, LGA, ward, polling unit, mobilisation group, and why you want to join.

03How we use it

  • To organise supporters by ward, LGA and state, and connect you with your local coordinators and WhatsApp groups.
  • To contact you by phone, SMS, WhatsApp or email about the movement — events, town halls, volunteering and voter information.
  • To follow up community issue reports and track whether problems get fixed.
  • To produce anonymous totals (for example, how many people have registered in a state). These never identify you.
  • To answer the questions you ask Ask FixNaija AI.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent fake or automated sign-ups.

We do not use your data for anything unrelated to these purposes, and we do not make automated decisions about you.

04Sensitive information

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, information about your political opinions, religious beliefs and ethnic origin is “sensitive personal data”. Registering with FixNaija reveals your support for the movement, and the registration form also asks about religion and ethnic group.

  • We process this information only with your explicit consent, which you give by ticking the consent box when you register.
  • We use religion and ethnic group only in aggregate — to make sure every community is represented and reached. We never use them to exclude or treat anyone differently.
  • Access is limited to authorised FixNaija administrators and coordinators who need it to organise.

05Our legal basis

We rely on your consent for registration, reports, volunteering and communications. You can withdraw consent at any time (see Your rights) — this doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew it. We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the website secure for basic technical logs.

06Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share it only with:

  • FixNaija coordinators in your ward, LGA or state, so they can reach you.
  • Service providers who run the website for us: Supabase (database), Vercel (website hosting), Google (Gemini AI for Ask FixNaija, and Google Fonts), and Meta/WhatsApp when you join a WhatsApp group or channel. They may process data on servers outside Nigeria; we choose providers with recognised security and data-protection safeguards.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires us to.

07How long we keep it

We keep your registration for as long as you remain part of the movement. If you ask us to delete it, we will do so within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep something. Issue reports are kept for as long as needed to follow up and show progress; after that we keep only anonymous statistics.

08How we protect it

  • The website uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Supporters' details can be read only by authorised administrators who sign in — the public website can submit information but cannot read it back.
  • We give coordinators access only to what they need.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will inform the Nigeria Data Protection Commission and affected people as the law requires.

09Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can:

  • See the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date;
  • Delete your data, or withdraw your consent at any time;
  • Object to or restrict how we use it, and ask for a copy in a portable format;
  • Complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you are unhappy with how we handled your data.

To use any of these rights, email fixnaijamovement@gmail.com with the phone number or email you registered with. We will reply within 30 days. To stop WhatsApp messages you can also simply leave the group or channel.

10Age limit

FixNaija registration is for Nigerians aged 18 and above — the voting age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will delete it.

11Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The website may use your browser's storage for simple features (for example, remembering a choice while you fill a form). Our hosting and font providers receive basic technical information when your browser loads the site.

12Changes & contact

We may update this policy as the movement and the website grow. We will change the “last updated” date above, and tell registered supporters about important changes.

Questions about your data?

Write to the FixNaija team — we read every message.
