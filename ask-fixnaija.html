Your civic questions, answered plainly.

Not sure of your rights? Wondering whose job a broken road is? Want to fact-check a claim about government? Ask FixNaija — a civic AI assistant trained to explain Nigerian governance, your rights, and how to take action, in clear language anyone can understand.

Hi 👋 I'm FixNaija, your civic assistant. Ask me about your rights, how Nigerian government works, how to get your PVC, how to report a problem in your area, or fact-check a civic claim. How can I help you today?
