The story

Building institutions, not chasing office

Born on 8 January 1972 in Ondo City, Ondo State, Prince Adewole Adebayo is a prince of the Ondo Kingdom, a lawyer, and an entrepreneur whose life's work has centred on building lasting institutions rather than pursuing political position for its own sake.

In 2002 he founded House of Law (Adewole Adebayo & Co.), a legal practice. In 2016 he established KAFTAN TV, building a media platform with national reach. As the Social Democratic Party (SDP) presidential candidate, he brought a message of competence, fairness and national repair to the Nigerian people.

But FixNaija is different from any campaign. It is, in his words, not a political party, a slogan, or a moment — it is a nationwide repair project, built by ordinary Nigerians, organised street by street, ward by ward, until no community is left behind. His role is to convene and serve it, not to own it.
