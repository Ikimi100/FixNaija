How It Works

Three Simple Steps

From your community to the corridors of power. Here's how your report makes an impact.

1

Report the Issue

Fill the form with your details, location, and a description of what needs fixing. Upload a video if you have one.

2

We Verify & Escalate

Our team reviews your report, verifies the details, and transmits it to the relevant unit of the Adebayo FixNaija Movement for appropriate action.

3

Track the Fix

We follow up on the status of your report and update you on progress. No report is forgotten — every voice counts.
