Ƙungiyar FixNaija · Adebayo–Bugaje 2027

Sabuwar Najeriya ta fara daga gare mu.

FixNaija ƙungiya ce ta ’yan Najeriya talakawa a dukkan Ƙananan Hukumomi 774. Muna tsarawa daga mazaɓa zuwa mazaɓa don zaɓen Yarima Adewole Adebayo da Usman M. Bugaje ranar Asabar, 16 ga Janairu 2027 — kuma mu riƙa bin diddigin ayyukan gwamnati.

Takarar 2027 · SDP

Kudu da Arewa a kan takardar zaɓe ɗaya.

Lauya kuma ɗan kasuwa daga Ondo, tare da tsohon ɗan majalisar tarayya daga Katsina — suna ɗauke da shiri ɗaya don yin bankwana da talauci da rashin tsaro.

Shugaban Ƙasa

Yarima Adewole Adebayo

Lauya, mai sasanta rikici kuma ɗan kasuwa, haifaffen birnin Ondo. Ya yi aiki a Hukumar Gaskiya da Sulhu ta Laberiya, ya kafa KAFTAN TV, kuma shi ne ya rubuta shirin “Bankwana da Talauci da Rashin Tsaro”.

Mataimakin Shugaban Ƙasa

Usman M. Bugaje

Mai kawo sauyi kuma tsohon ɗan Majalisar Wakilai ta Tarayya daga Jihar Katsina.

Bankwana da Talauci da Rashin Tsaro
