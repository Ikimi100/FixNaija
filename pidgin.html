FixNaija Movement · Adebayo–Bugaje 2027

New Naija go start from us.

FixNaija na movement of ordinary Naija people for all 774 Local Governments. We dey organise ward by ward to vote Prince Adewole Adebayo and Usman M. Bugaje on Saturday, 16 January 2027 — and to make government answer for wetin dem do.

The 2027 ticket · SDP

South and North for one ballot.

One lawyer and builder from Ondo, plus one former federal lawmaker from Katsina — dem carry one plan to say bye-bye to poverty and insecurity.

For President

Prince Adewole Adebayo

Lawyer, arbitrator and businessman wey dem born for Ondo City. E work for the Liberia Truth and Reconciliation Commission, e start KAFTAN TV, and na him write the “Farewell to Poverty and Insecurity” plan.

For Vice President

Usman M. Bugaje

Reformer and former member of the House of Representatives from Katsina State.
