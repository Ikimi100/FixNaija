Terms of Use

The short version

  • Use the site honestly: register only yourself, report real problems, and be respectful.
  • FixNaija is a citizen movement — not INEC and not the government. Always confirm official election information with INEC.
  • Answers from Ask FixNaija AI can be wrong. Treat them as a starting point, not legal advice.
  • How we handle your data is explained in our Privacy Policy.

01About these terms

These terms apply to everyone who visits or uses fixnaijamovement.com.ng. By using the website you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use the site.

02Who we are

The FixNaija Movement is a citizen-owned grassroots movement supporting Prince Adewole Adebayo, Presidential Candidate of the Social Democratic Party (SDP), and his running mate Usman M. Bugaje.

FixNaija is not the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC), a government agency or a polling authority. Information on this site about polling units, PVCs and election dates is provided to help you — always confirm it with INEC before relying on it.

03Using the site

When you use the website, you agree not to:

  • register other people, register more than once, or give false details;
  • pretend to be someone else, or pretend to speak for FixNaija, the SDP or its candidates;
  • post or send anything hateful, threatening, violent, or that stirs up ethnic or religious division;
  • send spam, or use bots or scripts to submit forms or collect data from the site;
  • try to break into, overload or interfere with the website or its database.

We may remove content, cancel registrations or block access where these rules are broken.

04Registrations & reports

  • Registration: you confirm that the details are yours, correct, and that you are 18 or older.
  • Issue reports: report real problems as honestly as you can. Don't include other people's private information, and don't upload photos of identifiable people without their permission.
  • By submitting a report or photo, you allow FixNaija to use it to follow up the problem and to share it (without your personal contact details) in our updates and campaigns.
  • We may edit, decline or remove submissions that are inaccurate, abusive or unsafe.

05Ask FixNaija AI

Ask FixNaija is an automated assistant. Its answers are generated by artificial intelligence and may be incomplete or wrong. They are for general civic information only and are not legal, financial or professional advice. Don't type sensitive personal details (like your PVC number or bank details) into the chat.

06Our content & logos

The FixNaija name, logo, text, photos and designs on this site belong to the FixNaija Movement or to their owners. The SDP name and logo belong to the Social Democratic Party. You are welcome to share our pages and posts to spread the word — please don't edit them in a way that changes their meaning or misleads people.

07Other websites

The site links to other services such as WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, X and TikTok. Those services have their own terms and privacy policies, and we are not responsible for them.

08No guarantees

We work hard to keep the website accurate and available, but it is provided “as is”. We can't promise it will always be error-free or online, and to the extent the law allows, FixNaija is not liable for losses that come from using the site or relying on its content.

09Changes & the law

We may update these terms from time to time; the “last updated” date above shows the latest version. These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
